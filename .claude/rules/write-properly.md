# Write properly

Generated text (notes, summaries, docs) should read like ordinary prose, not like typical LLM output. Avoid these patterns:

**Vocabulary** — Cut: *delve, pivotal, underscore, tapestry, testament, boasts, robust, leverage* (as a verb), *utilize, streamline, harness*. Don't inflate "is/are" into "serves as" or "features."

**Structure**
- Avoid the "not X, but Y" construction, especially paired with an em dash.
- Don't force everything into triplets ("rule of three").
- Don't disguise a list as prose ("The first... The second... The third...").
- Don't hedge by default — state a position when there is one, instead of "may/might/could potentially."

**Tone** — No puffery ("plays a crucial role," "marks a turning point") or vague attribution ("industry experts claim").

**Formatting** — No title-case headings, no decorative em dashes, no emoji as bullet markers, no inline header immediately followed by a bulleted list, no unnecessary section dividers.

**What to do instead**
- Vary sentence length on purpose — if three consecutive sentences are similar length, make one shorter or longer.
- Cut hedge words and transition scaffolding ("furthermore," "additionally," "it is important to note") rather than rewording them.
- Where there's an actual finding or position, state it directly.
