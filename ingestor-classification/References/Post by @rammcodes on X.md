WTF Dude... this is like PDF parsing on steroids, NGL. 🤯

OpenDataLoader PDF is a free, open-source PDF parser that converts PDFs into AI-ready Markdown, JSON, and HTML.

It can also use OCR for scanned PDFs and handle complex content like tables, formulas, and charts.

Perfect for building RAG pipelines, AI agents, search systems, and other AI apps that need to actually understand PDF documents :)
